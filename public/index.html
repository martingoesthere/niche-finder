Niche Finder

The best niches and video ideas, found automatically. Every number comes from real YouTube videos in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and you can open each video to check it.

How the scores work
Demand: do people watch this?
Based on the median views of the verified videos (the middle video: half got more, half got less). 1,000 views = 0, 1,000,000 views = 100. Higher is better.
Competition: how big are the channels you'd compete with?
Based on the median subscribers of the channels in the results. 1,000 subs = 0, 10,000,000 subs = 100. Lower is easier for a new channel.
Opportunity: can a small channel win here?
Of the videos from channels under 1M subscribers, the share that got more than twice as many views as the channel has subscribers. 30% or more = 100. Higher is better.
Freshness: are new channels making it?
The share of videos made by channels that are less than 6 months old. 20% or more = 100. Higher is better.
Fit
(Demand + (100 − Competition) + Opportunity + Freshness) ÷ 4. All four count the same. It's for sorting the list, not a promise.
High, Medium, Low
70 to 100 = High (or Hard for competition). 40 to 69 = Medium. 0 to 39 = Low (or Easy for competition). The small marks on each meter sit at 40 and 70.
Confidence
Low = 30 to 49 verified videos, medium = 50 to 99, high = 100 or more. Below 30 videos the app gives no score.
What is not included
Money (ad rates), AI content and faceless detection. Free YouTube data doesn't show them, so the app doesn't guess.
